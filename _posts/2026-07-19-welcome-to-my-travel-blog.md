In a few weeks I'll be setting off on a trip through Germany and Switzerland, and
I wanted to kick off this blog before I even leave — there's something worth
capturing in the anticipation itself, not just the memories afterward.

## Why this trip

I've wanted to see the contrast between Germany's mix of old-world cities and
modern energy, and Switzerland's mountains and lakes, for a long time. Pairing
the two felt right: a few days of city walking and history, then a shift into
alpine air and slower days.

## The rough plan

- **Germany** — starting in a major city to get over jet lag, then working
  through a couple of historic towns. Half the appeal is just wandering old
  streets with no fixed agenda.
- **Switzerland** — heading into the mountains for hiking and train rides with
  the kind of views that don't translate to photos. Planning to keep at least
  one day totally unplanned.

## What I'm most looking forward to

- Train travel between cities — I've heard the rail system in both countries
  makes it easy to just show up and go.
- Local food that has nothing to do with what shows up in guidebooks.
- Whatever ends up being the thing I didn't plan for.

## What I'm bringing (in short)

Packing light is the goal this time — one bag, layers for both city streets and
mountain trails, and as few "just in case" items as I can manage.
